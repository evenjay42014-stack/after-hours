# After Hours — first 10 conversations

One yes at $797/mo is $9,564/year. You need 10 owner conversations this week, not ads.

## Opener

"Quick question — when a no-AC call hits at 7:41pm, who answers it tonight?"

If they say voicemail or morning:

"That's the job your competitor is winning. I run after-hours coverage in your voice — missed calls, web forms, Google reviews. $797/mo. If we don't send 20 real replies in month 1, you don't pay."

Stop talking.

## Where the 10 come from

1–3. Google Maps: HVAC / plumber / electrician. Shops with 20–80 reviews and late or generic review replies.
4–6. Facebook groups: "[city] HVAC", contractor groups. Comment on "AC out" / "pipe burst" posts.
7–8. People you already know who own a truck and a Google listing.
9. The shop that didn't answer YOU after 5pm. That's the demo.
10. Ask every almost-yes: "Who else in town loses jobs at night?"

## Week 1

- Day 1: 10 Maps DMs + 5 group comments
- Day 2: 5 follow-ups + 2 in-person stops
- Day 3: deliver 3 mocked replies for anyone who sent missed-call notes
- Day 4–5: close one $497 install. Start coverage Friday night.

If nobody takes the retainer in 10 conversations, you talked to managers. Talk to owners who missed a call yesterday.

## Do not say

AI agency. Automation. Chatbot. We use Grok. I can also do your social media.

Say: after-hours coverage. In your voice. Same night.
